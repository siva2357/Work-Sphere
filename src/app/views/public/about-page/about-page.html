About Work-Sphere

Building the Operating System for Modern Workplaces

Work-Sphere was created to solve a fundamental disconnect in growing organizations: HR teams manage people in one system while managers and employees track daily projects, tasks, and requests in another. We unified both worlds into a single, role-aware platform.

Our Mission

Clarity Across Every Level

To empower organizations with transparent hierarchy management, effortless self-service HR, and real-time operational visibility—from executive leadership to every individual contributor.
